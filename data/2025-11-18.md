### [1] [An Analytical Model for the Eccentricity Cascade: Hot Jupiter Formation via S-type Instability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10726)
*Eritas Yang,Yubo Su*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文通过分析偏心级联机制，阐明了热木星形成的动力学过程，发现在内三重系统接近动力学不稳定时，原热木星的偏心率最有效地被激发，而第四个遥远天体的存在使得这种不稳定能够逐渐接近。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究热木星形成的高偏心率迁移机制，特别是通过von Zeipel-Lidov-Kozai循环，但传统机制需要行星初始轨道与伴星轨道几乎垂直的精细调节。本文旨在通过偏心级联机制规避这种精细调节需求。

Method: 通过分析动力学特性，推导S型不稳定性的解析准则，获得描述偏心级联起始的闭式表达式，并界定其有效运行的参数空间。

Result: 发现偏心级联机制在广泛的中间扰动体范围内都能有效运行，表明其作为稳健迁移通道的潜力。

Conclusion: 偏心级联机制为热木星形成提供了一个在广泛参数范围内有效的迁移通道，避免了传统机制所需的精细调节问题。

Abstract: A widely explored pathway for hot Jupiter (HJ) formation is high-eccentricity migration driven by von Zeipel-Lidov-Kozai cycles induced by an exterior companion. However, for a distant or low-mass companion, this mechanism typically demands that the planet's initial orbit be very nearly perpendicular to that of the companion. In previous work (Yang et al. 2025), we demonstrated that such fine-tuning can be circumvented in the HAT-P-7 system due to the presence of an intermediate body that efficiently couples the orbits of the planet and the distant companion -- a mechanism we termed the eccentricity cascade (EC). In this work, we analytically characterize the dynamics governing the EC and delineate the parameter space within which it effectively operates. Our qualitative results are as follows: (i) The proto-HJ's eccentricity is most efficiently excited when the inner triple is on the verge of dynamical instability, (ii) the addition of a distant fourth body allows this instability to be approached gradually, and (iii) the instability mechanism is closely related to the stability of circumstellar (S-type) planets in binaries. By deriving an analytic criterion for S-type instability, we obtain closed-form expressions describing the onset of the EC. Our results show that efficient HJ formation via the EC occurs across a broad range of intermediate perturbers, highlighting its potential as a robust migration channel.

### [2] [TESS Investigation -- Demographics of Young Exoplanets (TI-DYE) IV: a Jovian radius planet orbiting a 34 Myr Sun-like star in the Vela association](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.10734)
*Madyson G. Barber,Andrew W. Mann,Andrew Vanderburg,Khalid Barkaoui,Karen A. Collins,Sebastian Carrazco-Gaxiola,Phil Evans,Matthew J. Fields,Michael Gillon,Todd J. Henry,Katharine M. Hesse,Wei-Chun Jao,Emmanuel Jehin,Sydney Jenkins,Tim Johns,David R. Rodriguez,Richard P. Schwarz,William C. Storch,Cristilyn N. Watkins,Francis P. Wilkin*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 本文验证了TOI-6448 b这颗年轻行星的发现，它是一个8.8±0.8地球半径、轨道周期14.8天的行星，位于34±3百万年的Vela Population IV星团中，填补了年轻行星样本的空白。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 发现年龄小于50百万年、轨道周期小于30天的年轻行星对于理解开普勒发现的成熟行星分布的形成机制至关重要，但目前样本量仍然不足。

Method: 确认TOI-6448恒星属于Vela Population IV星团，并使用等龄线、变星观测和陀螺年代学重新推导星团年龄，验证行星TOI-6448 b的存在。

Result: 确认TOI-6448 b是一颗8.8±0.8地球半径、轨道周期14.8天的行星，其宿主恒星年龄为34±3百万年，位于参数空间中较少有较老行星的区域。

Conclusion: 这一发现支持了先前关于年轻恒星周围存在超额5-11地球半径行星的发现，未来在Vela、金牛-御夫、天蝎-半人马和猎户座等区域的持续搜索预计将揭示数十个年龄小于50百万年的凌星行星。

Abstract: The discovery of infant (< 50 Myr), close-in (<30-day period) planets is vital in understanding the formation mechanisms that lead to the distribution of mature transiting planets as discovered by Kepler. Despite several discoveries in this age bin, the sample is still too small for a robust statistical comparison to older planets. Here we report the validation of TOI-6448 b, an 8.8 +/- 0.8 Re planet on a 14.8 day orbit. TOI-6448 was previously identified to be a likely member of Vela Population IV. We confirm the star's membership and re-derive the age of the cluster using isochrones, variability, and gyrochronology. We find the star, and thus planet, to be 34 +/- 3 Myr. Like other young planets, TOI-6448 b lands in a region of parameter space with few older planets. While just one data point, this fits with prior findings of an excess of 5-11Re planets around young stars far beyond what can be explained by reduced sensitivity at young ages. Our ongoing search of Vela, Taurus-Auriga, Sco-Cen, and Orion are expected to reveal dozens more < 50 Myr transiting planets.
