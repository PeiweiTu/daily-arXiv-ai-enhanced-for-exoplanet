### [1] [Efficient Exoplanet Imaging Simulations of the Habitable Worlds Observatory](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15511)

Main category: astro-ph.IM

TL;DR: 本文开发了星冕仪的光学模型，使用Bluestein快速傅里叶变换(BFFT)技术高效模拟衍射光学，评估了60米星冕仪与HWO望远镜配合时的核心通量，结果显示分段离轴望远镜孔径可实现68%的核心通量。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 需要直接成像模拟来评估60米星冕仪在宜居世界观测站(HWO)中的补充作用，并表征行星探测性能和指导任务设计权衡。

Method: 开发星冕仪光学模型，引入Bluestein快速傅里叶变换(BFFT)技术来高效模拟衍射光学，在可见光到近红外波段(500-1000 nm)评估核心通量，使用6米分段和遮挡望远镜孔径。

Result: HWO星冕仪与分段离轴望远镜孔径配合，在0.7 λ/D半径的光度孔径内实现了68%的最佳核心通量；使用额外遮挡孔径时达到66%的核心通量。

Conclusion: BFFT技术能够高效进行衍射光学模拟，星冕仪与HWO望远镜配合可实现高核心通量，为系外行星探测任务提供了重要的光学性能评估工具。

Abstract: Direct imaging simulations of starshades and other proposed mission concepts are needed to characterize planet detection performance and inform mission design trades. In order to assess the complementary role of a 60 m starshade for the Habitable Worlds Observatory (HWO), we develop the optical model of a starshade and simulate solar system imaging at 0 degrees and 60 degrees inclinations. The optical core throughput of a direct-imaging system is a key metric that governs exposure time and the potential exoplanetary yield of a mission. We use our optical model to evaluate core throughput, incorporating 6 m segmented and obscured telescope apertures, over the visible to near-infrared wavelength band (500-1000 nm). Accurate diffractive optical simulations of this form require many large Fourier transforms, with prohibitive run-times, as both the starshade mask and telescope aperture require fine-scale spatial sampling. We introduce a Fourier sampling technique, the Bluestein Fast Fourier Transform (BFFT), to efficiently simulate diffractive optics and enable high-fidelity simulations of core throughput. By characterizing sampling requirements and comparing BFFT's computational complexity to standard Fourier methods (for example, DFT and FFT), we demonstrate its efficiency in our optical pipeline. These methods are implemented in PyStarshade (Taaki 2025), an open-source Python package offering flexible diffraction tools and imaging simulations for starshades. Our results show the HWO starshade used with a segmented off-axis telescope aperture achieves an optimal core throughput measured within a photometric aperture of radius 0.7 lambda/D of 68 percent. With an additionally obscured aperture, a 66 percent core throughput is achieved.

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 目前已知的年轻系外行星大多与其宿主恒星的自转轴对齐，但样本数量有限限制了统计结论。本研究旨在通过测量TIDYE-1 b的轨道倾角来扩展年轻行星系统的轨道对齐统计。

Method: 使用Rossiter-McLaughlin效应测量TIDYE-1 b的轨道倾角，结合同时期的光谱和测光数据来建模恒星活动趋势，并利用外部先验确定速度基线。

Result: 测得天空投影倾角|λ|=11.8^{+5.9°}_{-5.0}，结合恒星自转速度得到三维真实倾角ψ=15.2^{+7.3°}_{-5.7}，结果与对齐轨道一致。

Conclusion: TIDYE-1 b行星轨道与恒星自转轴基本对齐，即使其外围原行星盘存在错位，这表明行星可能保持与恒星对齐的状态，但需要更多观测来排除小概率的错位情况。

Abstract: Despite the wide range of planet-star (mis)alignments in the mature population of transiting exoplanets, the small number of known young transiting planets are nearly all aligned with the rotation axes of their host stars, as determined by the sky-projected obliquity angle. The small number of young systems with measured obliquities limits statistical conclusions. Here we determine the sky-projected obliquity ($λ$) of the 3 Myr transiting planet with a misaligned outer protoplanetary disk, TIDYE-1 b (IRAS 04125+2902 b), using the Rossiter-McLaughlin (RM) effect. Our dataset lacks a pre-transit baseline and ingress, complicating a blind RM fit. Instead, we use contemporaneous spectra and photometry from a mass-measurement campaign to model the stellar activity trend across the transit and provide an external prior on the velocity baseline. Our result is consistent with an aligned orbit, suggesting the planet remains aligned to its star even though the outer disk is misaligned, though additional RM observations are needed to exclude the low-probability tail of misaligned ($>30^{\circ}$) scenarios present in our posterior.

### [3] [Solar Periodic Companion and Random Stellar Flybys: Dynamical Perturbations of Highly Eccentric Comets in the Oort Cloud](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15121)
*Shahed Shayan Arani*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 该研究分析了盖亚望远镜数据揭示的恒星飞越对奥尔特云高偏心率彗星轨道的影响，发现单个恒星飞越可使彗星雨率增加约2倍，百万年内累积增加约40倍。同时识别出一个可能的太阳长期伴星候选体，其周期性接近可显著增强彗星雨活动。

  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 利用盖亚望远镜的新数据研究恒星飞越对奥尔特云高偏心率彗星轨道的影响，探索太阳系长期伴星存在的可能性及其对彗星动力学的塑造作用。

Method: 使用分析估计和直接N体模拟量化恒星飞越对高偏心率轨道的影响，并对盖亚DR3中所有具有六维相空间数据的恒星进行完整动力学搜索。

Abstract: The Gaia space telescope has transformed our understanding of random stellar encounters with the Solar System. This study examines how such perturbations influence the most eccentric comets in the Oort Cloud (OC), a thermalized reservoir of $\sim 10^{12}$ icy bodies extending from $10^{4}$ to $10^{5}$ AU. Recent Gaia-based analyses indicate about 20 stellar passages within 1 pc of the Sun per Myr. Using analytical estimates and direct $N$-body simulations, we quantify how these encounters modify highly eccentric orbits: individual flybys enhance comet-shower rates by factors of about 2, producing a cumulative increase of roughly 40 over a Myr. This star exhibits recurrent $\sim 45$ Myr perihelion passages and, in simulations, can elevate comet-shower activity by an order of magnitude. Because a perturber of this kind could also be a dispersed solar sibling, the companion hypothesis links the dynamical structure of the OC directly to the long-standing problem of identifying the Sun's birth-cluster relatives. The chemical-abundance pattern of such a star therefore becomes a critical observational test. Together, these results clarify how both stochastic encounters and potential long-period companions shape the dynamical evolution and observable output of the outermost OC, while highlighting the possibility that the OC retains a dynamical memory of the Sun's earliest stellar environment.

### [5] [A JWST Transit of a Jupiter Analog: II. A Search for Exomoons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.15317)
*David Kipping,Ben Cassese,Quentin Changeat,Daniel Yahalomi,Alex Teachey,Billy Edwards*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 使用JWST NIRSpec观测数据寻找系外卫星的研究，发现长期趋势对分析有重大影响，七个分析模型显示强系外卫星信号，但可能由恒星黑斑事件引起，需要2027年再次观测来消除歧义。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 利用JWST的卓越观测能力首次系统性地搜索系外卫星，特别是围绕木星大小的系外行星Kepler-167e的卫星。

Method: 使用NIRSpec JWST 60小时时间序列数据，比较仅行星模型和行星+卫星模型，采用三种不同数据处理流程和四种长期趋势建模方法（两种线性模型和两种高斯过程）。

Result: 七个分析模型显示强系外卫星检测信号，轨道大小约为行星的10%，但可能由恒星黑斑事件引起，与系外卫星信号高度相似。

Conclusion: JWST数据中的长期趋势对系外卫星搜索有深远影响，需要更深入理解这些系统误差的原因和最佳建模实践，建议2027年再次观测以消除歧义。

Abstract: We present a search for exomoons around the Jupiter-like exoplanet Kepler-167e using a NIRSpec JWST transit. Our 60 hour time series clearly reveals the enormous impact of long-term trends in NIRSpec data, specifically a gradual flux drift occurring over each of six 10 hour exposures. We weighed the evidence for exomoons by comparing a planet-only model with a planet+moon model for a grid of twelve different analysis choices. Our grid was comprised of three different reduction pipelines and four different models for the exposure-long trends - two using linear models and two using Gaussian processes. Seven grid realizations indicate a strong exomoon detection, typically favoring a Roche-skimming orbit roughly 10% the size of the planet. We find that the only likely real astrophysical feature driving these fits is a syzygy-like event occurring almost exactly mid-transit, which is highly ambiguous with a spot-crossing event. Indeed, we show that a spot of the necessary size is compatible with the earlier Kepler data. Ironically, the fact that JWST is so superior to Kepler means that our fits are effectively driven by a single transit - a regime in which exomoons have enormous freedom to explain non-Gaussian behavior. We thus strongly urge the next transit be observed in October 2027 to break these degeneracies. Our pilot study to seek transiting exomoons with JWST reveals the profound impact exposure-long trends exert - a cautionary tale for future analyzes of this data - as well as the need for a deeper understanding of this systematic's cause and modeling best-practices.

</details>
