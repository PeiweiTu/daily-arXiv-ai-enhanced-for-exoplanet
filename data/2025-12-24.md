### [1] [Breakthrough Listen Observations of 3I/ATLAS with the Green Bank Telescope at 1-12 GHz](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19763)
*Ben Jacobson-Bell,Steve Croft,Ellie White,Andrew P. V. Siemion,Matthew Lebofsky,David H. E. MacMahon*

Main category: astro-ph.IM

TL;DR: 对星际物体3I/ATLAS进行技术特征搜索，未检测到候选信号


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 星际物体3I/ATLAS接近地球，为寻找外星技术特征提供了独特机会

Method: 使用100米罗伯特·C·伯德绿岸望远镜在1-12 GHz频段进行技术特征搜索

Result: 未检测到候选信号，灵敏度达到100毫瓦水平

Conclusion: 对3I/ATLAS的技术特征搜索结果为阴性，但为未来星际物体观测提供了参考

Abstract: 3I/ATLAS, an interstellar object, made its closest approach to Earth on 2025 December 19. On 2025 December 18, the Breakthrough Listen program conducted a technosignature search toward 3I/ATLAS using the 100 m Robert C. Byrd Green Bank Telescope at 1-12 GHz. We report a nondetection of candidate signals down to the 100 mW level.

### [2] [The Solirad (So) as a Convenient Unit for Quoting Astronomical Irradiances for Planetary Insolations and Exoplanetary Instellations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.20126)
*Eric E. Mamajek,Jason T. Wright,Noah W. Tuchow,Patrick A. Young,Matthew A. Kenworthy,Emily A. Gilbert*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 论文提出使用"solirad"（So）作为太阳辐照度的新单位，1 So = 1361 W m⁻²，对应IAU 2015决议B3中定义的名义总太阳辐照度值。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 当前行星和系外行星科学文献中用于表示"通量"、"日照"、"辐照"等的单位往往繁琐或不一致，需要一个简化的单位来引用太阳辐照度。

Method: 引入新单位"solirad"（So），定义为1 So = 1361 W m⁻²，相当于IAU 2015决议B3中定义的名义总太阳辐照度值。

Result: 成功定义了solirad单位，该单位与IAU名义总太阳辐照度完全对应，并等同于视测热星等m_bol = -26.832 mag。

Conclusion: solirad单位简化了天文应用中辐照度的引用，为行星和系外行星研究提供了统一、简洁的辐照度单位。

Abstract: Measurements of physical parameters for stars and (exo)planets are often quoted in units normalized to the Sun and/or Earth. The nominal total solar irradiance, ${S}^{\rm N}_{\odot}$, while based on a current best estimate with uncertainties, was adopted to be an exact reference value of 1361 W m$^{-2}$ by IAU 2015 Resolution B3, corresponding to ``the mean total electromagnetic energy from the Sun, integrated over all wavelengths, incident per unit area per unit time at distance 1 au''. In the planetary and exoplanetary science literature, the units employed for ``flux'', ``insolation'', ``instellation'', etc., are often cumbersome or inconsistent. To simplify the quoting of irradiance units for astronomical applications, we introduce the portmanteau solirad, short for solar irradiance, as an abbreviated version of the longer IAU term ``nominal total solar irradiance''. The solirad (So) is a unit of irradiance, where 1 solirad = 1 So = 1361 W m$^{-2}$, equivalent to the IAU nominal total solar irradiance, and to an apparent bolometric magnitude of $m_{bol}$ = -26.832 mag (per IAU 2015 Resolution B2).

</details>
