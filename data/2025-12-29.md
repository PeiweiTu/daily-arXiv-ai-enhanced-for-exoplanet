  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 通过研究超热木星大气中的化学物种，特别是中性和电离金属元素，可以了解行星形成过程中的难熔元素丰度，为行星形成机制提供关键线索。

Method: 使用高分辨率光谱仪CARMENES和PEPSI观测HAT-P-70b的昼侧热发射光谱，通过交叉相关分析检测大气化学物种，并进行大气反演以约束热剖面和元素丰度。

This disequilibrium suggests that the atmosphere is likely undergoing significant hydrodynamic escaping, which enhances the atmospheric density at high altitudes where the ionic lines are formed.

### [2] [Episodic planetesimal disruptions triggered by dissipation of gas disk](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21493)
*Kang Shuai,Li-Yong Zhou,Hejiu Hui*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 行星盘气体耗散期间，木星和土星的长期共振与木星的平均运动共振共同触发了大量灾难性碰撞，解释了早期太阳系频繁碰撞的观测证据。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 解释早期太阳系（约1000万年内）频繁发生灾难性碰撞的观测证据，尽管星云气体通常会抑制星子偏心率从而减少碰撞频率，需要找到能够激发原行星盘的强动力学机制。

Method: 研究木星和土星在星云气体耗散过程中产生的长期共振扫掠效应，结合木星的平均运动共振，分析这些共振如何触发大量灾难性碰撞。特别关注长期共振位于约2-3天文单位时的碰撞事件。

Result: 长期共振和平均运动共振能够触发大量灾难性碰撞，这些碰撞在长期共振位于约2-3天文单位时呈阶段性发生，并在之后持续。气体耗散完成后，灾难性碰撞频率下降，行星胚胎散射成为主要碰撞驱动力。

Conclusion: 行星盘气体耗散期间，由长期共振和平均运动共振激发的剧烈环境在原行星盘中普遍存在，这解释了早期太阳系频繁碰撞的观测证据。

Abstract: Catastrophic disruptions of planetesimals occur in high-velocity collisions. Radioisotope dating of planetesimal disruption events recorded in meteorites confirms frequent catastrophic collisions in the first $\sim$10 Myr of the Solar System, reflecting a violent environment of the time. However, the nebula gas can damp the eccentricity of planetesimals and suppress the frequency of planetesimal collisions. Strong dynamical mechanisms that excited the protoplanetary disk are required. Here we show that the sweeping secular resonances of Jupiter and Saturn induced by the nebular gas dissipation, together with the mean motion resonances of Jupiter, can trigger a large number of catastrophic collisions, which occur episodically when the secular resonances are at $\sim$2-3 au and continue thereafter. After the gas dissipation completes, catastrophic collisions decrease in frequency, with scattering by planetary embryos becoming the major driving force of the collisions. Our results suggest that the violent environment excited by secular and mean motion resonances can be ubiquitous in protoplanetary disks during nebula dissipation.

### [3] [Numerical simulation of lunar response to gravitational waves and its 3D topographic effect using the spectral-element method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21667)
*Lei Zhang,Han Yan,Jinhai Zhang,Xian Chen*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 开发了高阶3D有限元方法模拟月球对低频引力波的响应，验证了方法准确性，发现月球地形对引力波信号有显著放大效应


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 月球作为天然的韦伯棒可以放大引力波信号，但缺乏3D数值模拟方法准确确定放大效应，需要开发可靠的计算方法

Method: 开发了高阶3D有限元方法（谱元法，SEM）来数值模拟月球对20 mHz以下引力波的响应，通过与半解析解比较验证方法准确性

Result: 方法验证显示频率偏差小于3%（第一个峰值约1 mHz）和小于0.8%（后续峰值至10 mHz）；发现月球3D地形效应在特定频率成分上增强了引力波信号

Conclusion: 月球表面地形对引力波响应具有不可忽视的影响，为全面评估月球对引力波的共振响应铺平道路，对月球引力波探测的战略规划具有重要意义

Abstract: The Moon has been regarded as a natural Weber bar capable of amplifying gravitational waves (GWs) for detecting events across a wide range of frequencies. However, accurately determining the amplification effects remains challenging due to the absence of 3D numerical simulation methods. In this study, we develop a high-order 3D finite element method (spectral-element method, SEM) to numerically simulate the lunar response to GWs below 20 mHz. We verify the accuracy of our method by comparing the resonant peaks of our results with those from semi-analytical solutions and find that the frequency deviation is less than 3% for the first peak at about 1 mHz and less than 0.8% for the subsequent peaks up to 10 mHz. Using this method, we evaluate the amplification of GW signals due to 3D topographic effects of the Moon, and we find enhancements at a series of specific frequency components. These results highlight the non-negligible effect of surface topography on the lunar response to GWs, as a fundamental factor that holds significant implications across both global and regional analyses. Our work paves the way for a comprehensive evaluation of the Moon's resonant response to GWs, helpful for the strategic planning of lunar GW detections.

*Rebecca Szabó,Valentin D. Ivanov,M. Švanda*

Main category: astro-ph.IM

TL;DR: 该论文指出目前约有70颗系外行星处于宜居带，其中50颗围绕M型恒星运行，这些恒星活动强烈。虽然行星与恒星的质量和光度比有利于观测，但恒星活动产生的UV和X射线辐射是否允许复杂生命存在仍是关键问题。需要大规模研究活动性恒星及其宜居行星，Wide Field Survey望远镜适合此研究。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>

Method: 论文提出需要开展大规模综合性研究，分析活动性恒星（特别是耀星）的特性及其对宜居行星的影响。研究将利用Wide Field Survey望远镜进行观测，该望远镜适合此类大规模巡天研究。

However, the lingering question remains whether the UV and X-ray emission, associated with the stellar activity, allows for complex life. A comprehensive study focused on properties of flaring exoplanet hosts and their activity, on a much larger scale than these few tens (soon to become hundreds) of stars with habitable planets is called for, to answer the question if such stars can harbor habitable planets. The proposed Wide Field Survey telescope is well suited for this study.

### [6] [An Enhanced "Flux-Corrected Transport"-Based Plasmasphere Refilling Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.21342)
*Jaden Fitzpatrick,Kausik Chatterjee,Naomi Maruyama*

Main category: physics.space-ph

TL;DR: 将多离子双流FCT流体模型扩展到包含自洽电子温度演化，取代恒温假设，更真实地模拟等离子层填充过程


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 改进等离子层填充模型，通过纳入自洽的电子温度演化来更准确地描述压力和双极电场梯度对离子输运的影响

Method: 扩展多离子双流FCT流体模型，求解电子能量方程以允许时空变化的温度，取代恒温假设

Result: 模型成功再现了两阶段填充行为、H+主导、早期O+贡献增强以及H+和He+通过双极电场的耦合；敏感性实验显示不同离子种类对填充轨迹的独特作用

Conclusion: 通过纳入更真实的温度变化，增强的模型加强了对地磁暴后等离子层恢复期间复杂多离子输运过程的物理理解，为未来扩展到三维几何提供了稳健框架

Abstract: A previously developed multi-ion, two-stream Flux-Corrected Transport (FCT) hydrodynamic model for plasmasphere refilling has been extended to incorporate self-consistent electron temperature evolution. The past assumption of a constant temperature along the modeled flux tube has been replaced by solving the electron energy equation, permitting spatially and temporally varying temperature. This improvement provides a more physically complete representation of the pressure and ambipolar electric-field gradients that influence ion transport. The extended model allows us to investigate two-stage refilling behavior established by prior observations and simulations. The model continues to reproduce the expected dominance of H+, enhanced early-time O+ contributions, and the coupling between H+ and He+ through the ambipolar electric field during the transition between stages. Sensitivity experiments with modified initial ion concentrations, including cases representing seasonal effects, highlight the distinct roles of each ion species in shaping the refilling trajectory. Comparisons across L-shells 3 and 4 further confirm the robustness of the model framework for future extension to three-dimensional geometries. Overall, by incorporating more realistic temperature variations, this enhanced model strengthens the physical understanding for interpreting complex multi-ion transport processes during plasmasphere recovery following geomagnetic storms.
