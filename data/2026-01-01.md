### [1] [Atmospheric Mass Flux as a Function of Ionospheric Emission on Unmagnetized Earth](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24004)
*P. C. Hinton,D. A. Brain,N. R. Schnepf,R. Jarvinen,J. Cessna,F. Bagenal*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 研究无磁场类地行星的离子逃逸和太阳离子沉积，发现太阳离子沉积率可能与行星离子逃逸率相当，大气质量在10亿年内减少不到3%，表明地球的固有磁场对大气保留可能并非必需。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索无磁场类地行星的大气演化机制，特别是离子逃逸和太阳离子沉积之间的平衡关系，评估地球固有磁场对大气保留的必要性。

Method: 使用RHybrid离子动力学电子流体代码模拟无磁场地球与太阳风的全球等离子体相互作用，通过改变全球电离层发射率来量化行星离子逃逸率（O⁺和H⁺）和太阳风沉积率（H⁺）。

Result: 在典型太阳风条件下（v_sw=400 km/s，n_sw=5 cm⁻³），太阳离子沉积率可能与行星离子逃逸率相当；大气质量在10亿年内减少不到3%。

Conclusion: 地球的固有磁场可能对大气保留并非必需，电离层发射可能随时间演化趋向净质量通量为零的临界发射率。

Abstract: We explore ion escape from, and solar ion deposition to, \hll{an unmagnetized Earth-like planet}. We use RHybrid, an ion-kinetic electron-fluid code to simulate the global plasma interaction of unmagnetized Earth with the solar wind. We vary the global ionospheric emission rate, and quantify the resultant planetary ion escape rates ($O^+$ and $H^+$) and the solar wind deposition rate ($H^+$). We use these results to compute the net mass flux to the atmosphere and find that the solar ion deposition rate could be comparable to planetary ion escape rates. For the emission rates simulated, our results show that under typical solar wind conditions ($v_{sw} = 400 \ km \ s^{-1}$, $n_{sw} = 5 \ cm^{-3}$), the mass of the atmosphere would decrease by less than 3\% over a billion years, indicating that Earth's intrinsic magnetic field may be unnecessary for retention of its atmosphere. Lastly, we present a hypothesis suggesting that ionospheric emission may evolve through time towards a critical emission rate that occurs at a net mass flux of zero.

### [2] [Seafloor Weathering and Stochastic Outgassing Unlikely to Significantly Shorten the Future Lifespan of Earth's Terrestrial Biosphere](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24538)
*Livia Zhu,R. J. Graham,Dorian S. Abbot*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 该研究通过扩展气候-大陆风化耦合模型，考察海底风化和CO₂脱气率随机变化对地球陆地生物圈剩余寿命的影响，发现强海底风化可能缩短寿命但仍有超过10亿年，而随机波动影响有限，支持了陆地生物圈寿命延长至16-18.6亿年的结论。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 先前研究认为太阳变亮会导致地球碳酸盐-硅酸盐循环通过吸收CO₂来抵消温度上升，最终导致陆地植物因过热或CO₂匮乏而灭绝。传统估计陆地生物圈寿命约10亿年，但最新研究修正为16-18.6亿年。本研究旨在探讨海底风化和CO₂脱气率随机变化对这一估计的影响。

Method: 扩展了现有的气候-大陆风化耦合模型，纳入海底风化过程和CO₂脱气率的随机变化。使用最新的参数约束，分析不同情景下陆地生物圈寿命的变化，包括强海底风化、弱海底风化以及不同规模的随机波动。

Result: 1. 如果海底风化反馈强于大陆风化且占全球硅酸盐风化的大部分，陆地生物圈寿命可能缩短，但仍很可能超过10亿年；2. CO₂脱气率的随机波动只有在超过过去10亿年观测到的规模时才有显著影响；3. 弱海底风化和低变异性随机性的影响较小。

Conclusion: 研究初步支持地球陆地生物圈寿命延长的结论，表明这一延长寿命对可能在外行星间变化的行星参数具有鲁棒性，同时指出海底风化是需要进一步研究和约束的关键过程。

Abstract: Current understanding suggests that as the Sun brightens in the far future, Earth's carbonate-silicate cycle will offset increasing temperatures by drawing CO$_2$ out of the atmosphere, ultimately leading to the extinction of all terrestrial plant life via either overheating or CO$_2$ starvation. Most previous estimates put the future lifespan of Earth's terrestrial biosphere at $\sim$1 billion yr, but recent work used a new coupled climate-continental weathering model with up-to-date parameter constraints to revise this estimate upward to 1.6-1.86 billion yr. In this study, we extend the model to examine the impacts of seafloor weathering and stochastic variations in CO$_2$ outgassing rates on the remaining lifespan of Earth's terrestrial biosphere. We find that if seafloor weathering has a stronger feedback than continental weathering and accounts for a large portion of global silicate weathering, then the remaining lifespan of the terrestrial biosphere can be shortened, but a lifespan of more than 1 billion yr (Gyr) remains likely. Similarly, stochastic fluctuations in outgassing rates can have a significant impact if the size of the fluctuations exceed those observed over the last 1 billion yr. The impact of weak seafloor weathering and lower variability stochasticity are minor. Our work provisionally supports a lengthened lifespan of Earth's terrestrial biosphere, suggests robustness of this lengthened lifespan to planetary parameters that may vary among exoplanets, and identifies seafloor weathering as a key process that requires further study and constraint.

### [3] [Pair Space in Classical Mechanics III. Some Four-Body Central Configurations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.23730)
*Alon Drory*

Main category: math-ph

TL;DR: 研究四体问题中的中心构型，使用新的对空间形式得到向量方程，分析至少两对体间距离相等的子类解，发现只有四面体、风筝形和等腰梯形三种非共线构型，其具体形状由质量比决定。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究四体问题中的中心构型，即所有天体受力都指向空间中固定单点的特殊构型，这对于理解天体力学中的稳定运动模式具有重要意义。

Method: 采用新提出的对空间形式，得到完全描述中心构型的向量方程组，然后研究至少两对体间距离相等的子类解。

Result: 发现至少两对体间距离相等的非共线构型只有三种：四面体（唯一的非平面构型）、风筝形和等腰梯形。这些构型的具体形状（内角）由天体的质量比决定，并给出了所有相关关系的数学表达式。

Conclusion: 在四体问题中，至少两对体间距离相等的中心构型只有三种基本几何形状，其具体构型完全由质量比决定，这为理解多体系统的稳定构型提供了新的理论框架。

Abstract: We study central configurations in the four body problem, i.e., configurations in which the forces on all the bodies point to a fixed, single point in space. The newly formulated pair-space formalism yields a set of vectorial equations that fully characterize such configurations. We investigate a sub-class of solutions in which at least two pairs of inter-body distances are equal. The only such non-collinear configurations are the tetrahedron (the unique non-planar configuration), kites and the isosceles trapezium. The specific shapes (internal angles) are determined by the ratio of the masses of the bodies. Mathematical expression are given for all these relations.

### [4] [Turbulence-Driven Corrugation of Collisionless Fast-Magnetosonic Shocks](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.24425)
*Immanuel Christopher Jebaraj,Mikhail Malkov,Nicolas Wijsen,Jens Pomoell,Vladimir Krasnoselskikh,Nina Dresing,Rami Vainio*

Main category: astro-ph.SR

TL;DR: 该研究开发了线性MHD框架分析上游湍流如何导致无碰撞快磁声激波表面产生波纹，建立了入射湍流谱与波纹振幅、漂移和相干尺度的映射关系。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 观测显示激波表面存在有组织的波纹，而传统理论将激波视为平滑平面边界。上游湍流可能是驱动波纹的机制，这会影响粒子注入和辐射条件，需要建立理论框架来理解这一现象。

Method: 采用线性MHD方法，将激波视为移动界面而非固定边界，通过有效阻抗概念总结激波响应，上游湍流仅通过其统计特性输入，建立了从入射谱到波纹特性的映射关系。

Result: 当透射的下游快模在激波坐标系中几乎平行于激波传播时，响应最大，产生由下游法向群速控制的洛伦兹型增强。研究了压缩比、等离子体β值和倾斜度对波纹特性的影响。

Conclusion: 该框架为理解激波表面波纹提供了实用工具，有助于解释日球层和超新星遗迹激波发射中的精细结构，波纹特性受激波几何和等离子体参数共同影响。

Abstract: Collisionless fast-magnetosonic shocks are often treated as smooth, planar boundaries, yet observations point to organized corrugation of the shock surface. A plausible driver is upstream turbulence. Broadband fluctuations arriving at the front can continually wrinkle it, changing the local shock geometry and, in turn, conditions for particle injection and radiation. We develop a linear-MHD formulation that treats the shock as a moving interface rather than a fixed boundary. In this approach the shock response can be summarized by an effective impedance determined by the Rankine-Hugoniot base state and the shock geometry, while the upstream turbulence enters only through its statistics. This provides a practical mapping from an assumed incident spectrum to the corrugation amplitude, its drift along the surface, and a coherence scale set by weak damping or leakage. The response is largest when the transmitted downstream fast mode propagates nearly parallel to the shock in the shock frame, which produces a Lorentzian-type enhancement controlled by the downstream normal group speed. We examine how compression, plasma $β$, and obliquity affect these corrugation properties and discuss implications for fine structure in heliospheric and supernova-remnant shock emission.

</details>
