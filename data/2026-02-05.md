We provide further insights into the "latitude effect", showing that solar eclipses occur most frequently around the Arctic and Antarctic Circles. We also show how the gradual shift of aphelion and perihelion with respect to the seasons produces a $\sim$21,000-year cycle in the frequency of eclipses in the Northern and Southern Hemispheres.
