Motivation: 研究中晚期M型矮星周围的行星分布特征，特别是验证半径谷（Radius Valley）在最低质量恒星周围是否消失，以及探索M型矮星周围行星的组成特性。

Method: 使用TESS观测的8134颗中晚期M型矮星数据，通过定制化流水线进行系统搜索，采用注入-恢复方法评估探测灵敏度，测量行星发生率随轨道周期、辐照度和行星半径的变化。

Result: 发现77个经过验证的凌星行星候选体；测量到30天内半径>1R⊕的行星累积发生率为1.10±0.16每星；发现单峰行星半径分布（峰值1.25±0.05R⊕），半径谷消失；测得超级地球与亚海王星比例为5.5:1；未发现热木星，设定了上限0.012每星（10天内）。

Conclusion: 中晚期M型矮星周围的行星半径分布呈现单峰特征，半径谷消失，支持M型矮星周围亚海王星是富水世界的理论预测，M型矮星成为小型近距行星最丰富的宿主恒星。

Abstract: We present the deepest systematic search for planets around mid-to-late M dwarfs to date. We have surveyed 8134 mid-to-late M dwarfs observed by TESS with a custom built pipeline and recover 77 vetted transiting planet candidates. We characterize the sensitivity of our survey via injection-recovery and measure the occurrence rate of planets as a function of orbital period, instellation, and planet radius. We measure a cumulative occurrence rate of $1.10\pm0.16$ planets per star with radii $>1\, R_\oplus$ orbiting within 30 days. This value is consistent with the cumulative occurrence rate around early M dwarfs, making M dwarfs collectively the most prolific hosts of small close-in planets. Unlike the bimodal Radius Valley exhibited by close-in planet population around FGK and early M dwarfs, we recover a unimodal planet radius distribution peaking at $1.25\pm0.05 \, R_\oplus$. We additionally find $0.954\pm0.147$ super-Earths and $0.148\pm0.045$ sub-Neptunes per star, with super-Earths outnumbering sub-Neptunes 5.5:1, firmly demonstrating that the Radius Valley disappears around the lowest mass stars. The dearth of sub-Neptunes around mid-to-late M dwarfs is consistent with predictions from water-rich pebble accretion models that predict a fading Radius Valley with decreasing stellar mass. Our results support the emerging idea that the sub-Neptune population around M dwarfs is composed of water-rich worlds. We find no hot Jupiters in our survey and set an upper limit of 0.012 hot Jupiters per mid-to-late M dwarf within 10 days.
