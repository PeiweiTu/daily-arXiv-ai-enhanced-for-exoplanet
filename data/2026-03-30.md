### [1] [The role of inner disk edges in shaping ultra-short-period planet systems around late M dwarfs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26204)
*S. N. Brandenberger,M. Sanchez,N. Van der Marel,A. A. Vidotto,Y. Miguel*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 该研究通过N体模拟探讨超短周期岩石行星的形成机制，发现行星盘内边缘的位置对USP行星的形成至关重要，只有固定近边缘和向内演化边缘两种情景能够产生USP行星。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 超短周期岩石行星（轨道周期小于1天）的形成机制尚不明确，研究旨在探究其形成和存活是否与行星盘内边缘的位置有关，考虑不同的盘边缘模型。

Method: 使用N体模拟，包含行星-盘相互作用、恒星-行星潮汐相互作用和相对论修正。模拟月球质量的行星种子在低粘度盘（α_t=10^-4）中通过卵石吸积生长。盘内边缘采用三种模型：固定近边缘、由磁层截断半径设定的向外演化边缘、由共转半径定义的向内演化边缘。

Result: USP行星的形成受盘内边缘位置严格控制。只有固定近边缘情景和向内演化边缘情景能够产生USP行星，因为行星倾向于跟随盘内边缘的运动。这表明当内边缘保持在快速旋转恒星的共转半径附近时，USP行星的形成更有利。

Conclusion: USP行星的形成与行星盘内边缘的位置密切相关，特别是当内边缘保持在快速旋转恒星的共转半径附近时，有利于USP行星的形成和存活。

Abstract: Close-in rocky planets are the most common type of exoplanets around late M dwarfs, ranging from more temperate worlds to highly irradiated lava planets with molten surfaces, and many theoretical studies have attempted to explain their formation. However, the origin of rocky planets with orbital periods shorter than one day, known as ultra-short-period (USP) planets, remains uncertain. We aim to investigate whether the formation and survival of USP planets is connected to the location of the inner edge of the protoplanetary disk, considering different disk edge prescriptions. We use N-body simulations that include planet-disk interactions, star-planet tidal interactions, and relativistic corrections, applied to a sample of lunar-mass planetary seeds growing via pebble accretion in a low-viscosity disk ($α_t = 10^{-4}$). The inner edge of the disk is modeled in three ways: as a fixed close-in edge, as an outward-evolving edge set by the magnetospheric truncation radius, and as an inward-evolving edge defined by the corotation radius. USP planet formation appears to be tightly controlled by the location of the disk's inner edge. Our simulations show that only the close-in-fixed-edge Scenario and the inward-evolving-edge Scenario are capable of producing USP planets, as planets tend to follow the movement of the disk's inner edge. This suggests that USP planet formation is favored when the inner edge remains close to the corotation radius of a rapidly rotating star.

### [2] [The atmosphere of K2-18 b: The role of hazes, clouds and photoelectrons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26474)
*P. Lavvas,R. Liu,G. Tinetti,S. Paraskevaidou,P. Drossart,A. Coustenis*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 该研究使用自洽的正向模型分析温带亚海王星K2-18 b的大气特征，首次评估了光电子对系外行星大气化学的影响，发现高金属丰度（200-400倍太阳）的富氢大气能一致再现JWST观测的透射光谱。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: K2-18 b作为围绕M矮星运行的温带亚海王星，已成为JWST观测的基准案例。研究旨在通过自洽模型约束其金属丰度、成分和热结构，特别关注非平衡化学、光化学霾和云的作用，并首次评估光电子对系外行星大气化学的影响。

Method: 使用自洽的正向模型，探索广泛的金属丰度和本征温度范围，评估霾和云的形成，将生成的透射光谱与多个独立数据处理流程的JWST观测结果进行比较。首次在系外行星大气化学中评估光电子的影响。

Result: 高金属丰度（200-400倍太阳）的富氢大气能一致再现观测到的透射光谱，与数据处理流程无关。大气成分受非平衡化学强烈影响，CH4主导光谱，CO2和OCS有贡献，中红外波长可能有C2H4贡献。光电子增强了几种非平衡物种（特别是含氮分子）的产生。光化学霾在塑造热结构中起关键作用，在10-100毫巴范围产生温度最小值，促进H2O凝结，抑制其气态丰度。在足够强的霾冷却下，NH4SH凝结为观测光谱中NH3的明显缺失提供了自然解释。

Conclusion: K2-18 b的高金属丰度富氢大气模型能很好地解释JWST观测数据，非平衡化学和光化学过程对大气成分和热结构有重要影响。无需考虑超出本研究范围的额外分子物种即可再现观测光谱。

Abstract: The atmospheric characterisation of temperate exoplanets is becoming accessible with JWST, providing a critical connection between Solar System planets and the more commonly observed hot-Jupiters. K2-18 b, a temperate sub-Neptune orbiting an M dwarf, has emerged as a benchmark case following extensive JWST observations and ongoing debate regarding its atmospheric composition. We investigate K2-18 b using a self-consistent forward model to constrain its metallicity, composition, and thermal structure, with particular emphasis on the role of disequilibrium chemistry, photochemical hazes and clouds. For the first time in this context, we also assess the impact of photoelectrons on the atmospheric chemistry of an exoplanet. We explore a wide range of metallicities and intrinsic temperatures, evaluate haze and cloud formation, and compare the resulting transmission spectra with available JWST observations from multiple independent pipelines. We find that a high metallicity (200-400xsolar) H2-rich atmosphere consistently reproduces the observed transit spectra, independent of the data reduction pipeline used. The atmospheric composition is strongly shaped by disequilibrium chemistry, with CH4 dominating the spectrum alongside contributions from CO2 and OCS, and a potential contribution from C2H4 at mid-infrared wavelengths. Photoelectrons enhance the production of several disequilibrium species, particularly nitrogen-bearing molecules. Photochemical hazes play a key role in shaping the thermal structure, producing a temperature minimum near the 10-100 mbar range that enables efficient H2O condensation, suppressing its gaseous abundance. Under sufficiently strong haze cooling, NH4SH condensation provides a natural explanation for the apparent absence of NH3 in the observed spectra. No additional molecular species beyond those considered here are required to reproduce the observed spectra.
