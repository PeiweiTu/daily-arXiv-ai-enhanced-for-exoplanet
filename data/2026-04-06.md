Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 对彗星D/2021 A1 (Leonard)在近日点前的挥发性物质排放进行多波段观测研究，发现HCN和CS的混合比随日心距离变化呈现不同模式，揭示了彗核活动与分布源产生的差异。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究彗星D/2021 A1 (Leonard)在接近太阳过程中挥发性物质的演化特征，通过多波段、多时相观测来理解彗核活动、物质释放机制以及彗星结构变化对化学成分的影响。

Method: 使用北方扩展毫米波阵列(NOEMA)和阿塔卡马探路者实验(APEX)望远镜，在2021年11月5日至12月10日期间对彗星进行多时相观测，覆盖日心距离从1.3到0.80天文单位。检测HCN和CS分子，并对CH₃OH、H₂CO和CO设置3σ上限。通过谱线运动学和空间约束分析分子释放机制。

Conclusion: 彗星D/2021 A1的挥发性物质演化反映了复杂的物理过程，包括太阳辐射驱动的活动和彗星结构破坏的影响。HCN和CS的不同行为表明它们具有不同的产生机制。研究强调了多时相、多仪器监测对于捕捉彗星快速、物种依赖性变化的重要性。

We securely detected HCN and CS and place 3$σ$ upper limits on CH$_3$OH, H$_2$CO, and CO abundances. Line kinematics and NOEMA spatial constraints indicate that HCN was released at or near the nucleus (parent scale length $<300$ km), while CS showed higher gas expansion velocities and mixing ratios that increased with decreasing $r_H$ $-$ consistent with production from a distributed source. ### [2] [A 1151-Year Quasi-Commensurability Among the Pre-Uranian Planets: Empirical Detection and Statistical Characterization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03049)
*Carlos Baiget Orts*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 在太阳系中发现了一个多行星准共振周期T*=420,403天（约1,151年），该周期使水星、金星、地球、火星、木星和土星这六颗天王星发现前的行星同时回到近似位置，平均角位移仅14.7度。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 探索太阳系行星轨道是否存在长期准周期共振模式，特别是天王星发现前已知的六颗行星之间是否存在协同运动模式。

Method: 使用DE441星历表计算六颗行星（水星、金星、地球、火星、木星、土星）的日心黄经，在±1,300年的对称搜索范围内进行穷举计算，应用序列比较相似性度量寻找全局最小值。

Result: 发现T*=420,403天（约1,151年）为全局最优周期，此时六颗行星的平均同时角位移仅为14.7度，标准差0.75度，该结果在1,200年的参考历元范围内稳定，且不受序列长度影响。

Conclusion: 太阳系存在一个1,151年的多行星准共振周期，该周期与巴比伦天文学家已知的金星周期相同，但首次证明该周期对所有六颗前天王星行星同时最优，具有重要的天体力学意义。

Abstract: We report the empirical detection of a multi-planet quasi-commensurability in the Solar System. A computational exhaustive search identifies T* = 420,403 days (~1,151 years) as the global minimum of a series-comparison similarity metric applied to daily heliocentric ecliptic longitudes of the six pre-Uranian planets (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn -- the planets known before the discovery of Uranus in 1781), computed from the DE441 ephemeris over a symmetric search range of +-1,300 years. At this interval, the mean simultaneous angular displacement of all six planets from their positions T* days earlier is 14.7 degrees, with a standard deviation of 0.75 degrees sustained over a century-long comparison window, and stable across any reference epoch spanning 1,200 years. The score of the optimal cycle lies 3.26 sigma below the mean of all 2,600 candidates, with every other candidate producing a worse score. The result is independent of the reference epoch and of the series length (T* is the global minimum for series as short as one year). No sub-multiple produces a comparable result, establishing T* as an irreducible primary quasi-period. The interval 1,151 years was known to Babylonian astronomers as the period after which Venus returns to the same position in the sky (de Jong 2019). The present work demonstrates that this same interval is simultaneously optimal for all six pre-Uranian planets, a property not previously documented in the astronomical literature. Geocentric consequences -- including the quasi-synchronisation of planetary retrograde motions -- are discussed. Source code and data are publicly available.

### [3] [CO and N2 Produced from H2O, CO2, and NH3 Cometary Ice Analogs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03207)
*Alexandra McKinnon,Alexia Simon,Michelle R. Brann,Elettra L. Piacentino,Karin I. Oberg,Mahesh Rajappan*

Main category: astro-ph.EP

TL;DR: 该研究探索彗星中超挥发物CO和N₂的光化学起源，发现紫外辐射和电子轰击冰模拟物可产生与彗星观测值相当的N₂，但CO主要来自低温捕获。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 彗星中CO和N₂等超挥发物的来源存在争议：传统认为来自低温冻结/捕获，但本研究探索另一种可能机制——低挥发性前体物质的光解作用，以更准确解释彗星形成条件。

Method: 使用CO₂、NH₃、H₂O:CO₂、H₂O:NH₃和H₂O:CO₂:NH₃等彗星冰模拟物，在10-100K温度下进行紫外辐射和电子轰击实验，测量生成的CO和N₂产量及其与水的比例。

Result: 所有光处理冰中均检测到CO和N₂形成：CO/H₂O为0.4-0.9%，N₂/H₂O为0.03-0.7%；CO/CO₂混合比为2.5-62%，N₂/NH₃为0.7-9%。与彗星观测值比较显示，多数彗星N₂可归因于NH₃光解，而CO主要来自低温捕获。

Conclusion: 彗星N₂丰度很可能源于NH₃在冰中的光解作用，因此N₂/H₂O比值<1%时需谨慎推断彗星形成位置；而CO丰度主要反映低温捕获过程，仍可用于约束彗星形成温度条件。

Abstract: Hypervolatile species such as carbon monoxide (CO) and molecular nitrogen (N2) have been detected in comets, and could be used to constrain comet formation temperature conditions if their presence is due to freeze-out and/or entrapment. Here we instead explore another plausible origin of cometary hypervolatiles: photodissociation of less volatile species. We characterize CO and N2 formation following ultraviolet (UV) irradiation and electron bombardment of carbon dioxide (CO2), ammonia (NH3), H2O:CO2, H2O:NH3, and H2O:CO2:NH3 cometary ice analogs. We find that CO and N2 form in all photoprocessed ices at temperatures between 10 K and 100 K, resulting in 0.4-0.9 % CO and 0.03-0.7 % N2 relative to water, and CO/CO2 and N2/NH3 mixing ratios of 2.5-62 % and 0.7-9 %, respectively, across the experiments. Because our initial ices are reasonably well-matched to interstellar ices and we use UV exposure similar to a dark cloud, we can compare the resulting ratios directly to cometary abundances. Such a comparison shows that while only a few of CO observations in comets are readily explained by photodissociation, almost all observed cometary N2 can be accounted for by photodissociation of NH3 embedded in water ice. The latter result is also consistent with observed similarly elevated isotopic ratios of N2 and NH3 in 67P. Taken together, our results suggest that N2/H2O ratios less than 1 % should be used cautiously when inferring a comet's formation location, while the more substantial CO abundances seen in many comets do likely imply entrapment at low ice temperatures.

### [4] [Proton Temperature Anisotropy Across Interplanetary Shocks: A Statistical Analysis with WIND observations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.02452)
*Zeping Jin,Lingling Zhao,Xingyu Zhu,Vladimir Flosinski,Gary P. Zank,Jakobus Le Roux,Yiming Jiao,Ashok Silwal,Nibuna S. M. Subashchandar*

Main category: physics.space-ph

TL;DR: 对1997-2024年Wind卫星观测的约800个行星际激波进行统计分析，研究质子温度各向异性随激波几何结构、压缩比和距离的变化规律。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 行星际激波能有效改变太阳风质子温度各向异性，但不同几何结构激波的具体影响机制尚不明确，需要系统研究激波如何调控质子温度各向异性及其背后的物理过程。

Method: 使用Wind卫星1997-2024年观测的约800个行星际激波数据，统计分析上游和下游质子温度各向异性，研究其与激波几何结构（准垂直vs准平行）、压缩比、距激波距离的依赖关系，并与CGL双绝热模型进行比较。

Result: 1) 准垂直激波下游产生显著的垂直温度增强(Tperp>Tpara)，准平行激波下游保持近各向同性；2) CGL模型在准垂直激波高估垂直加热、低估平行加热，准平行激波则相反，表明非绝热过程的重要性；3) 激波驱动的各向异性在激波附近高度局域化，随距离增加逐渐弛豫；4) 下游各向异性受动力学不稳定性调控：准垂直激波受质子回旋和镜模不稳定性约束，准平行激波受平行火蛇不稳定性限制。

Conclusion: 行星际激波处的温度各向异性演化受激波几何结构、局域物理过程和动力学不稳定性调控的共同控制，CGL模型存在系统偏差，需要包含非绝热过程才能准确描述激波加热机制。

Abstract: Interplanetary (IP) shocks efficiently modify the proton temperature anisotropy of the solar wind. Analyzing ~800 IP shocks observed by the Wind spacecraft from 1997-2024, we present a statistical study of upstream and downstream proton temperature anisotropy and its dependence on shock geometry, compression, and distance from the shock. We find that (1) quasi-perpendicular shocks produce a pronounced enhancement of perpendicular temperature downstream (Tperp > Tpara), whereas parallel shocks remain near isotropic downstream due to typically stronger upstream Tpara; (2) comparisons with the Chew-Goldberger-Low (CGL) double-adiabatic model reveal geometry-dependent deviations. Together, these results show that the evolution of temperature anisotropy at interplanetary shocks is controlled by shock geometry, localized processes, and instability driven regulation.
